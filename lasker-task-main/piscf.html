Participant Information and Consent Form

Behavioural Study on Decision-Making and Learning

General Information

We appreciate your interest in participating in this online study. You have been invited to participate as you are healthy, over 18 years of age and with sufficient English language skills to understand the task instructions. You also do not have a diagnosis of a neurological or psychological condition.

We, researchers from the Institute of Cognitive Science at the University of Osnabrück, are investigating how people learn and make decisions in uncertain environments.

In the task, you will play a computer game in which you control a shield to protect the Earth from radiation beams. You will respond using the keyboard. The study consists of two sessions of approximately 30 minutes each, which you can complete with a short break in between. You will receive 1 VP (Versuchspersonenstunde) for your participation via SONA.

Voluntary Participation

Your participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any point by closing the browser window. Withdrawing will not result in any disadvantage, though you will not receive course credit if you do not complete the study.

Data and Privacy

Your data will be stored anonymously in a password-protected file and may be used in academic publications. No personal data that could identify you will be stored. Research data will be kept for a minimum of five years after publication.

We would like your permission to use your anonymised data in future studies and to share it with other researchers. Any information that could identify you will be removed before data is shared or results are made public.
